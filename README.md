# GDPR & Privacy Information Management (ISO 27701) Compliance Checklist

For small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) processing sensitive personal, customer, or employee data, establishing verifiable privacy governance is essential. This compliance checklist is built upon the unique "Auditor's Eye" methodology, which interprets the "grey areas" of compliance and simulates the exact scrutiny you will face during a formal certification audit to ensure there are no surprises. 

### 1. Privacy Information Management Systems (PIMS) Foundation
*   **Implement ISO/IEC 27701 Alignment:** Ensure your data privacy framework operates as a rigorous Privacy Information Management System (PIMS) aligned with ISO/IEC 27701. 
*   **Define PII Controls:** Establish and enforce explicit controls for both the Controllers and Processors of Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
*   **Execute DPIAs:** Conduct comprehensive Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) to govern data privacy and evaluate PII processing terms.

### 2. Operational & Technical Governance
*   **Align with Statutory Frameworks:** Ensure your technical architecture, cloud hosting models, and API integrations align directly with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
*   **Eliminate PII Risk:** Conduct technical forensic audits and risk treatments to actively identify and eliminate PII risks or vulnerabilities within your systems before they go live.
*   **Compliant Personnel Screening:** Ensure that all employment background checks and pre-employment screening processes are strictly compliant with data protection legislation.

### 3. Third-Party & Supply Chain Risk Management
*   **Audit Vendor Statements of Work (SOW):** Apply deep-dive scrutiny to third-party vendor SOWs, Service Level Agreements (SLAs), and engineering delivery pipelines to ensure external partners meet strict data privacy standards.
*   **Verify Data Protection Agreements:** Assess vendor compliance by evaluating their specific data protection agreements and privacy terms during procurement. 
*   **Prevent Third-Party Liability:** Scrutinize supplier relationships to prevent data leaks and regulatory breach fines stemming from non-compliant third-party code libraries. 

### 4. Audit-Ready Documentation Practices
*   **Reject Generic Templates:** Eliminate generic, boilerplate "copy-paste" documentation, as external auditors will reject templates that do not reflect actual operational workflows. 
*   **Tailor Compliance Files:** Ensure every policy, standard operating procedure, and risk treatment matrix is custom-authored to satisfy mandatory statutory requirements and directly reflect how your business functions.
*   **Maintain Independent Evidence:** Build an audit-ready evidence trail that ensures all technical deliverables and privacy controls are fully documented and verifiable by external assessors.
